# Intermediate Strength Standards

This document outlines the male intermediate-strength standards for one-rep max and the equivalent 7-rep numbers.

These are based on the [Weightlifting Strength Standards](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards).

The equivalent 7-rep numbers are good for lifters who don’t want to push for one-rep max numbers.
