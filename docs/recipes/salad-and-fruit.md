# Salad and Fruit

This is a small spinach salad with an orange and a kiwi on the side.  No cooking, about 3 minutes to make.

It makes one serving with around 200 calories.  It's designed to cover vitamin C, vitamin K, and vitamin A, which are hard to get from cooked meals.

## Ingredients

* 70g raw spinach
* 130g orange (1 medium)
* 70g kiwi (1 medium)
* 10ml olive oil (9g)
* 15ml lime juice (half a lime)

## Instructions

1. Put the spinach in a bowl.  Drizzle with the olive oil and lime juice and toss.
2. Peel the orange and kiwi and eat them on the side, or slice them into the salad.

Kale or arugula work in place of the spinach.  Any citrus works in place of the orange.

## Micronutrients

Here's what this meal adds, with the percentage of the adult male RDA:

* Vitamin C: about 155mg (170%).  Mostly from the orange and kiwi.
* Vitamin K: about 370mcg (300%).  Almost all from the spinach.
* Vitamin A: about 345mcg (40%).  Almost all from the spinach.
* Vitamin E: about 4mg (25%).  Split between the spinach, kiwi, and olive oil.
* Folate: about 190mcg (50%).  Mostly from the spinach and orange.

Some key takeaways:

* The olive oil is there for more than flavor.  Vitamins A, K, and E are fat-soluble, so a little fat with the spinach helps you absorb them.
* The fruit carries nearly all the sugar, and all of it is naturally occurring.
* Spinach is high in oxalates, which bind some of the calcium and iron.  The vitamin C from the fruit improves iron absorption.
