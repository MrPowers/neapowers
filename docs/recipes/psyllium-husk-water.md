# Psyllium Husk Water

This is psyllium husk stirred into water.  Two ingredients, about 1 minute to make.

It adds 7.5 grams of fiber for 20 calories.

## Ingredients

* 10g psyllium husk
* 300ml water

## Instructions

1. Pour the water into a glass.
2. Stir in the psyllium husk.
3. Drink it right away.  It thickens into a gel within a minute or two.
4. Follow it with another glass of water.

If you're new to psyllium, start with 5g and work up to 10g over a week.  Always take it with plenty of water.

Some key takeaways:

* Nearly all the carbs are fiber.  Psyllium husk is about 75% fiber by weight and has no sugar.
* Roughly 70% of the fiber is soluble, about 5 grams per glass.  Soluble fiber forms a gel in the gut and is the type that lowers LDL cholesterol.
* The 20 calories are mostly from fiber fermentation and are negligible.
