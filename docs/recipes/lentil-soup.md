# Lentil Soup

This is a dead-simple lentil soup made in a stovetop pressure cooker.  Three ingredients, one pot, about 30 minutes start to finish.

The whole batch is around 1,625 calories with 101 grams of protein and 48 grams of fiber.  It makes roughly 4 big bowls.

## Ingredients

* 400g dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
* 110g onion (1 medium), diced
* 120g carrots (2 medium), diced
* 15ml olive oil (14g)
* 2000ml water
* 9g salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in the pressure cooker over medium heat.  Add the onion and carrots and cook for 5 minutes until the onion softens.
2. Add the lentils, water, and salt.  Stir once.
3. Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over high heat.
4. Once at pressure, lower the heat to maintain pressure and cook for 10 minutes.
5. Turn off the heat and let the pressure release naturally (about 10 minutes).  Don't quick release, the starchy liquid can spit through the valve.
6. Open the lid, stir, and add more salt to taste.

Don't fill the pressure cooker more than halfway.  Lentils foam up and expand as they cook.

Split into 4 bowls, each bowl is about 406 calories, 69 grams of carbs, 4.6 grams of sugar, 25 grams of protein, 4.7 grams of fat (0.7 grams saturated), 12 grams of fiber, and 913mg of sodium.

Some key takeaways:

* Nearly all the protein and fiber come from the lentils.  The onion and carrots are mostly for flavor.
* One bowl covers 40% of the recommended 30 grams of daily fiber.
* Sugar is low at under 5 grams per bowl, and all of it is naturally occurring from the vegetables and lentils.
* The soup is very low in fat.  Skip the olive oil and the whole pot drops to about 4.6 grams of fat and 0.7 grams of saturated fat.
* Almost all the sodium comes from the salt.  Use 5g of salt instead of 9g and each bowl drops to about 520mg of sodium.
