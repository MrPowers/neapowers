# Chicken Stew

This is a very simple chicken stew made in a stovetop pressure cooker.  Three ingredients, one pot, about 25 minutes start to finish.

The whole batch is around 910 calories with 137 grams of protein.  It makes roughly 3 servings.

## Ingredients

* 600g boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into 4-5cm chunks
* 110g onion (1 medium), diced
* 123g tomato (1 medium), diced
* 15ml olive oil (14g)
* 250ml water
* 6g salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in the pressure cooker over medium heat.  Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until it softens.
2. Add the tomato and cook for 1 more minute.
3. Add the chicken, water, and salt.  Stir once.
4. Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over high heat.
5. Once at pressure, lower the heat to maintain pressure and cook for 6 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then quick release the rest.
7. Open the lid, stir, and add more salt to taste.  The chicken can be shredded with two forks if you want a pulled texture.

Split into 3 servings, each serving is about 303 calories, 5 grams of carbs, 2.6 grams of sugar, 46 grams of protein, 10 grams of fat (1.8 grams saturated), 1.1 grams of fiber, and 880mg of sodium.

Some key takeaways:

* The chicken provides nearly all the protein.  600g of breast gives you 135 grams of protein.
* About half the fat comes from the olive oil.  Skip the oil and the whole batch drops to about 785 calories and 16 grams of fat.
* Almost all the sodium comes from the salt.  Use 4g of salt instead of 6g and each serving drops to about 620mg of sodium.
