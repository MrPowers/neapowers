# Salmon Stew

This is a very simple salmon stew made in a stovetop pressure cooker.  About 15 minutes start to finish.

It makes one large serving with around 720 calories and 53 grams of protein.

## Ingredients

* 250g salmon fillet, skin removed, cut into 4-5cm chunks
* 110g onion (1 medium), diced
* 123g tomato (1 medium), diced
* 30ml lime juice (1 lime)
* 15ml olive oil (14g)
* 250ml water
* 3g salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in the pressure cooker over medium heat.  Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until it softens.
2. Add the tomato and cook for 1 more minute.
3. Add the water, salt, and the salmon.  The fish does not need to be submerged.
4. Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over high heat.
5. Once at pressure, lower the heat to maintain pressure and cook for 3 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and quick release the pressure.
7. Open the lid, gently stir, and squeeze in the lime juice.  Add more salt to taste.

The lime goes in at the end.  Cooking it under pressure dulls the flavor.

If the salmon is frozen, cook for 5 minutes instead of 3.

Some key takeaways:

* The salmon provides nearly all the protein and most of the fat.  Farmed Atlantic salmon is a fatty fish, so 250g carries about 33 grams of fat on its own.
* Skip the olive oil and the stew drops to about 595 calories and 34 grams of fat.  The salmon has enough fat to cook the onion without it.
* Almost all the sodium comes from the salt.  Use 2g of salt instead of 3g and the sodium drops to about 945mg.
