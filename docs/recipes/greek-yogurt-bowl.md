# Greek Yogurt Bowl

This is a two-ingredient Greek yogurt bowl.  About 1 minute to make.

It makes one serving with around 175 calories and 24 grams of protein.

## Ingredients

* 200g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
* 10g pumpkin seeds

## Instructions

1. Spoon the Greek yogurt into a bowl.
2. Sprinkle the pumpkin seeds on top.

Some key takeaways:

* The Greek yogurt provides nearly all the protein.  200g of nonfat Greek yogurt gives you about 21 grams of protein for under 120 calories.
* All the sugar is naturally occurring lactose from the yogurt.  Flavored or sweetened Greek yogurt will add a lot more.
* The pumpkin seeds carry most of the fat.  Full-fat Greek yogurt instead of nonfat adds about 10 grams of fat and 80 calories to the bowl.
* The numbers above are USDA values for plain nonfat Greek yogurt.  Brands vary, so check the label.
