# Oat Bowl

This is a simple oat bowl with four ingredients and no cooking required.  About 5 minutes to make.

It makes one serving with around 650 calories, 17 grams of protein, and 15 grams of fiber.

## Ingredients

* 70g rolled oats
* 20g salted almonds
* 150g banana (1 large), sliced
* 200ml chocolate oat milk

## Instructions

1. Put the oats in a bowl and pour the chocolate oat milk over them.
2. Let the oats soak for 5 minutes, or cover and refrigerate overnight for a thicker texture.
3. Top with the sliced banana and the almonds.

If you prefer it warm, microwave the oats and oat milk for 2 minutes before adding the toppings.

Some key takeaways:

* The oats provide most of the protein and fiber.  70g of oats gives you 9 grams of protein and 7 grams of fiber.
* The banana and chocolate oat milk account for nearly all the sugar.  Swap the chocolate oat milk for unsweetened oat milk and the sugar drops to about 21 grams.
* The almonds carry more than half the fat.  They also account for most of the sodium since they're salted.
* Chocolate oat milk varies a lot by brand.  The numbers above are for a typical brand with about 65 calories per 100ml.  Check the label and adjust if yours is different.
