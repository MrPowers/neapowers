# Tilapia Stew

This is a very simple tilapia stew made in a stovetop pressure cooker.  The fish goes in frozen, so there is no thawing or planning ahead.  About 15 minutes start to finish.

It makes one large serving with around 390 calories and 43 grams of protein.

## Ingredients

* 200g frozen tilapia fillet
* 110g onion (1 medium), diced
* 123g tomato (1 medium), diced
* 30ml lime juice (1 lime)
* 15ml olive oil (14g)
* 250ml water
* 3g salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in the pressure cooker over medium heat.  Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until it softens.
2. Add the tomato and cook for 1 more minute.
3. Add the water, salt, and the frozen tilapia.  The fish does not need to be submerged.
4. Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over high heat.
5. Once at pressure, lower the heat to maintain pressure and cook for 4 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and quick release the pressure.  Fish doesn't foam, so a quick release is fine.
7. Open the lid, break the fish into chunks with a spoon, and squeeze in the lime juice.  Add more salt to taste.

The lime goes in at the end.  Cooking it under pressure dulls the flavor.

Some key takeaways:

* The tilapia provides nearly all the protein.  200g of fish gives you 40 grams of protein for under 200 calories.
* Most of the fat comes from the olive oil, not the fish.  Skip the oil and the stew drops to about 265 calories and 3.8 grams of fat.
* Almost all the sodium comes from the salt.  Use 2g of salt instead of 3g and the sodium drops to about 900mg.
