# Músculo

This is a very simple músculo (beef shank) made in a stovetop pressure cooker.  Two ingredients, one pot, about 1 hour start to finish.

Músculo is a tough, lean cut full of connective tissue.  The pressure cooker breaks it down into tender, shreddable meat.

The whole batch is around 810 calories with 109 grams of protein.  It makes roughly 3 servings.

## Ingredients

* 500g músculo, cut into 4-5cm chunks
* 110g onion (1 medium), diced
* 15ml olive oil (14g)
* 500ml water
* 5g salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in the pressure cooker over high heat.  Add the músculo and brown it for 5 minutes, turning once or twice.
2. Add the onion and cook for 2 minutes until it softens.
3. Add the water and salt.  Stir once.
4. Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over high heat.
5. Once at pressure, lower the heat to maintain pressure and cook for 35 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and let the pressure release naturally (about 15 minutes).
7. Open the lid and check the meat.  It should pull apart easily with a fork.  If it's still tough, lock the lid and cook for another 10 minutes at high pressure.
8. Add more salt to taste.

Split into 3 servings, each serving is about 269 calories, 3.4 grams of carbs, 1.6 grams of sugar, 36 grams of protein, 11.5 grams of fat (3.2 grams saturated), 0.6 grams of fiber, and 756mg of sodium.

Some key takeaways:

* The músculo provides nearly all the protein.  500g gives you 108 grams of protein.
* Most of the fat comes from the beef itself.  Skip the olive oil and the whole batch drops to about 685 calories and 20.6 grams of fat.
* Almost all the sodium comes from the salt.  Use 3g of salt instead of 5g and each serving drops to about 495mg of sodium.
